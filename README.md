## Personalised mRNA vaccine for malignant melanoma

*Mariella Metzner & Olivia Hank*

Two 3D models with educational videos explain how a personalised mRNA vaccine trains the immune system against melanoma. One model shows the process at the cellular level, the other at the molecular level.

▶ [Cellular level](https://synthetic-physiology.github.io/ComputationalPhysiology2026/SciCom/MMOH_cellular.html) · [Molecular level](https://synthetic-physiology.github.io/ComputationalPhysiology2026/SciCom/MMOH_molecular.html)

## Aquagenic pruritus

*Liliana Rudolf*

An interactive model of how beta-alanine modulates aquagenic pruritus, the itch triggered by contact with water. Users vary the timing, the dose and the contact duration before water exposure (t = 0) and follow the itch intensity in arbitrary units (a.u.).

▶ [Open the model](https://synthetic-physiology.github.io/ComputationalPhysiology2026/AquaPur/Model01.html)

## Blood flow in a vascular network

*Florian Winter*

Block a vessel and watch the flow redistribute. The model shows how an occlusion changes blood flow in the neighbouring vessels of a network.

▶ [Open the model](https://synthetic-physiology.github.io/ComputationalPhysiology2026/BloodFlow/blood_flow_demo.html)

## Protein folding and protein–protein interaction

*Magali Stecher*

The CYBA p.Q143* mutation cuts the protein p22phox short. Interactive py3Dmol molecular models and schematic figures show that the truncated protein loses its C-terminal binding site for p47phox. Without this contact the NADPH oxidase complex and its catalytic core NOX2 cannot be activated. The antimicrobial respiratory burst fails, which leads to chronic granulomatous disease (CGD). The project also models the healthy mechanism: phosphorylation of the p47phox C-terminus exposes its SH3 domain, which then binds p22phox.

▶ [Project folder](InterFold)

## Insulin response to GLP-1 receptor agonists

*Luke Gemünden*

GLP-1 receptor agonists such as semaglutide reduce fat, but they also reduce muscle. A physiological glucose–insulin model built from published study values separates the effect of the drug into fat loss, muscle loss and direct drug actions. Users choose a patient group (healthy, obese, prediabetic, type 2 diabetes), adjust fat loss, muscle loss and strength training, and watch the oral glucose tolerance test change.

▶ [Open the simulator](https://synthetic-physiology.github.io/ComputationalPhysiology2026/MucleGLP1/MuscleGLP1.html)

## Piezo1 simulation

*Ben Seibel*

A molecular model of the mechanosensitive ion channel Piezo1, with and without mutations. It simulates the ion current when a whole cell or a membrane patch is stretched or pressed.

▶ [Open the simulator](https://synthetic-physiology.github.io/ComputationalPhysiology2026/PiezoSim/PIEZO1_Simulator.html)

## Myelopoiesis, APL and ATRA therapy

*Ben Glück*

A spatial agent-based model of the bone marrow simulates healthy granulopoiesis cell by cell. It then adds acute promyelocytic leukaemia (APL), driven by the PML-RARα fusion protein, and its treatment with all-trans retinoic acid (ATRA), which forces the leukaemic cells to differentiate. Users watch the marrow fill with blasts, start ATRA, and follow how the clonal diversity of healthy stem cells changes.

▶ [Open the simulation](https://synthetic-physiology.github.io/ComputationalPhysiology2026/Hematopoiesis_APL/HematopoiesisAPL.html)
