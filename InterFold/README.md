<h2 style="color: #1d5fa7; font-weight: 600; letter-spacing: 0.3px; margin-bottom: 10px;">
Structural and Functional Effects of the CYBA p.Q143* Truncation on p22phox and NADPH Oxidase Activation
</h2>
<p>
This project was created as part of the course <em>Computational Physiology</em> and investigates p22phox (encoded by the CYBA gene) and the p.Q143* nonsense mutation, which introduces a premature stop codon after position 142 and truncates the 195-amino-acid protein.
</p>
<p>
Using interactive py3Dmol molecular models and schematic visualizations, the project illustrates how losing the C-terminal region removes a key binding interface for p47phox, impairing activation of the NADPH oxidase complex and its catalytic core (NOX2), reducing the antimicrobial respiratory burst, and linking to chronic granulomatous disease (CGD).
</p>
<p>
It also models the healthy activation mechanism in which phosphorylation of the p47phox C-terminus exposes its SH3 domain to bind p22phox.
</p>
